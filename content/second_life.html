Bouldering › La Cerra › 2nd Life
It takes about 30 minutes to get to the 2nd Life sector from the campsite. The boulders are around 3 to 4 meters high and offer very good drop zones almost without exception. In the overview map, boulders are indicated that have not been climbed yet but have potential for new routes. Because of many trees, all boulders are in the shade and it can also get quite windy up here. If you boulder from Fb 5a to 7a, 2nd Life is a highly recommended sector with a lot of powerful problems all worth a try! 2nd Patchwork Baby is a superb problem, with an interesting start and a tricky finish to get to the topout. Since all boulders are on flat terrain, it is very good to stay here with children as well. The sector can also be reached quite easily from the day parking area.
