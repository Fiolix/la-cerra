Privacy Policy

This page explains which personal data are processed when you use the La Cerra website.

1. Controller

Required before publication:

Name or legal entity

Public privacy contact email

2. Hosting through GitHub Pages

The public website is delivered through GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub. When the website is accessed, technical connection data such as the IP address, browser information, requested files and time of access may be processed by the hosting provider to deliver and protect the service.

GitHub Privacy Statement

3. User accounts and Supabase

User authentication and the website database are provided through Supabase. Account data include the email address, username, technical account identifiers and authentication information. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are not visible to the website operator.

When you use personal features, the website stores information associated with your account, including ticklist entries, flashes, personal projects and profile settings.

Supabase Privacy Policy

4. Guestbook

Guestbook posts and replies are stored together with the author account, publication status and creation time. The displayed username and message are publicly visible unless an entry is hidden or deleted by a moderator.

Please do not publish private contact details or sensitive personal information in the guestbook.

5. Interactive maps and location

The interactive maps use MapLibre and map tiles from OpenFreeMap. Loading map tiles establishes a connection to the map provider, which can receive technical connection data such as your IP address and browser information.

The optional “Show my location” function requires browser permission. Your position is used in your browser to display your location on the map and is not stored in the La Cerra database. You can withdraw location permission through your browser settings.

OpenFreeMap

6. Local browser storage

The website uses browser storage for functions such as maintaining the authenticated session, remembering the last opened page and restoring navigation state. Authentication tokens stored by Supabase are required to keep you signed in. The website currently does not use advertising or analytics trackers of its own.

7. Contact by email

If you contact the operator by email, the information you provide is processed to answer your request and to handle any necessary follow-up.

8. Purpose, legal basis and retention

Account and feature data are processed to provide the requested website functions. Security-related technical data may be processed to maintain a reliable and secure service. Contact data are processed to answer enquiries. Exact legal bases and retention periods must be finalised together with the controller information before publication.

Required before publication: final legal bases under Article 6 GDPR, retention periods and any applicable third-country transfer information.

9. Your rights

Subject to the applicable legal requirements, you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Where processing is based on consent, consent can be withdrawn for the future. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection supervisory authority.

Required before publication: competent supervisory authority and complete contact route for privacy requests.

10. Updates

This privacy policy should be reviewed whenever services, providers or data-processing functions are changed.
