Grading, Shortcuts & Symbols

We used the Fb-scale for grading. We never thought that defining the grade of a route would be so complicated. Also, please keep in mind that many different people have opened up routes in La Cerra and this can cause additional discrepancies. Please understand the rating as an idea.

If you have problems finding your way, use the GPS indication on the map of the given sector. There is always a central point marked for it, the GPS coordinates are also on the map.

If there is an arrowhead on the route line, the topout is not visible on the picture. Information on where the route tops out can usually be found in the route description.

Routes with a sit start are marked by SD. Here, your buttocks is the last to leave the ground or crash pad. Only holds below the short vertical line may be used for the sit start.

Routes with standing start are drawn without a vertical baseline.

Projects are shown as a dashed line in the picture. If you complete a project, let us know and we will update it in the next guidebook.

Fine dotted lines in the images indicate which areas may not be used for a route.

(trav.)

Traverses are marked with the abbreviation (trav.). In the case of traverses, the line drawn in represents the handline.

Hints & Rules

  • All lines are meant to be topped out.
  • Only one block may be used for climbing the problem. If adjacent blocks under the start may also be used, it is indicated.
  • Please respect nature! No loud music, no littering! If you find rubbish, take it back with you!
  • Use the toilets provided. If they are too far away, bury your excrements and toilet paper. Wet wipes take forever to rot. Take them back with you and throw them in the garbage can.
  • Use only existing trails & paths.
  • Clean the stone from chalk after climbing, especially the tickmarks!
  • We did a lot of cleaning, but still sometimes there might be lichen on the stone. Take one of the steel brushes from the Crashpad room and help us keep the blocks free.
  • Do not create any foot- or handholds!
  • Do not block any paths when parking!
  • Check out this great initiative: savebouldering.eu
