Plan your visit

Conditions, topo symbols and the essential rules for bouldering at La Cerra.

When to Come

Spring and autumn usually offer the best conditions. Winter is generally mild, although rain and occasional cold nights are possible.

Summer can be extremely hot. Early or late sessions may still work, but it is not the best season for demanding projects.

Grades, Shortcuts & Symbols

Grades use the Fontainebleau scale and are suggestions only. They may feel different depending on conditions, body size and climbing style.

GPS: marks a central reference point for finding the sector.

Arrowhead: the topout continues beyond the visible image.

SD: sit start. Only holds below the short start line may be used.

Standing start: shown without a vertical start line.

Dashed route: an open project without a confirmed ascent.

Fine dotted line: rock outside the line may not be used.

Traverse: the drawn line represents the hand line.

Hints & Rules

  • All routes are intended to be topped out. Adjacent blocks may only be used when stated.
  • Respect nature: keep noise low, take all rubbish with you and use existing paths.
  • Use the provided toilets. If that is impossible, bury waste and take wet wipes away.
  • Brush off chalk and tick marks. Help keep lichen away without damaging the rock.
  • Never create or alter handholds or footholds.
  • Keep roads and paths clear when parking.
  • Learn more about responsible access at savebouldering.eu.

Safety

Bouldering is dangerous. Check landings and loose rock, use suitable crash pads and spotters, and climb at your own risk.

Hunting may take place in the area. Ask locally whether any sectors or approaches should be avoided.
