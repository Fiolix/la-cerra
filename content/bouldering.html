Bouldering on Sardinia

Sardinia is best known for sport climbing and multi-pitch routes. Although numerous bouldering areas are spread across the island, detailed information can still be difficult to find.

In the south, boulders close to Torre delle Stelle are worth several days of climbing. Nuoro and its surroundings offer smaller areas in the centre of the island, including limestone that gives your skin a break from the granite. Local climbers remain the best source of current information. Many areas are not climbed frequently, so bringing a good brush and allowing time for careful cleaning is a good idea.

Bouldering in Gallura

Rock, stones and boulders are everywhere on Sardinia, but the landscape of Gallura is something special. Its rugged granite offers countless possibilities that are still waiting to be explored.

Besides La Cerra, the developed bouldering area near Luogosanto is well worth a visit and lies roughly 20 minutes away. More boulders can be found around Arzachena and on the island of La Maddalena.
