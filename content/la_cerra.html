Bouldering › La Cerra
La Cerra is a broad granite bouldering area in northern Sardinia, set around Agriturismo La Cerra and below the distinctive Monte Pulchiana. Its sectors range from easily accessible blocks near the accommodation to extensive, adventurous areas in the surrounding landscape. The sharp granite offers varied climbing, and the area still has considerable potential for new problems.

Brief History

How it all began

La Cerra's bouldering potential has been known for some time. In 2013, La Sportiva uncovered and first climbed a number of problems during its Sardinia Bloc Scouting project. Inspired by these boulders, Eadan Cunningham came to La Cerra a year later and developed a large area next to Monte Pulchiana.

In 2019, Florian Schiffer and friends found another nearby area and named it 2nd Life. A small PDF guide by inseltrek.de also described some of the blocks in the area.

Our part

Attracted by the La Sportiva guide, the authors of the printed La Cerra topo arrived in November 2020. Together with Alberto and other climbers, they cleaned blocks, removed loose rock and established new lines. Sarah and Valle helped to clean and climb the Sushi-Free sector.

What began as a small guidebook project grew with support from Martin and Petra of tmms-shop, information generously shared by Eadan Cunningham, and the work of Florian Schiffer and the Stuntwerk team, who opened another sector in March 2021.

A look into the future

The surrounding terrain, particularly along the green and red hiking trails, still offers considerable potential. New developments and changes to existing problems can be shared through the website's guestbook.

When to Come

Summers on Sardinia can be extremely hot, which makes bouldering more exhausting. The sharp granite can still offer reasonable friction and a climbing day can be combined with time at the nearby beaches, but summer is not the best season for demanding projects.

Spring and autumn are ideal for a bouldering trip to La Cerra. Winters are generally mild and snow is rare, although several rainy days are possible and night-time temperatures can occasionally fall below zero.

Accommodation

Many of the blocks lie close to Agriturismo La Cerra. Visitors can stay with a van or tent under the cork oaks and use the campsite facilities. Guest rooms with private bathrooms are also available.

Crash pads and brushes may be available on site, and breakfast or dinner prepared with local ingredients can be arranged. For current availability and prices, please check agriturismolacerra.it or ask the hosts directly.

Day Parking

Day visitors are asked to use the designated parking area. Several small parking bays are available along the road. Please do not block the road; if the nearest bay is occupied, use another bay and allow a few extra minutes for the approach.

How to Get There

Sardinia has three international airports: Cagliari in the south, Alghero in the north-west and Olbia in the north-east. The drive from Olbia to La Cerra takes approximately one hour. A car is recommended because public transport in the area is limited.

Travellers bringing their own vehicle can reach Sardinia by ferry from mainland Italy, Sicily, France, Corsica or Spain. The most useful arrival ports include Olbia, Porto Torres and Cagliari.

Rest Days and Other Activities

La Cerra

The granite can be rough on the skin, so rest days are a useful part of any longer visit. Around La Cerra you can hike near Monte Pulchiana, explore the marked trails, take a mountain-bike trip or simply enjoy the peace of the agriturismo.

Monte Pulchiana

The prominent granite monolith offers wide views over the countryside and the bouldering sectors. A challenging trail reaches the back of the summit and is partly protected by fixed ropes. Route finding can be difficult, so local information and suitable experience are important.

Capo Testa

Capo Testa lies near Sardinia's northern tip and is known for remarkable coastal granite formations. The Valle di Luna offers a scenic half-day walk, while the peninsula can also be explored by canoe. Expect more visitors during the main holiday season.

Costa Paradiso

Costa Paradiso can be reached from La Cerra in roughly 40 minutes. It combines swimming and snorkelling with coastal walking, granite exploration and a few deep-water-soloing possibilities.

Lago del Liscia

About 30 minutes away by car, Lago del Liscia offers easy walks, birdwatching and a very different landscape. Nearby, the Olivastri Millenari include ancient olive trees believed to be several thousand years old.
