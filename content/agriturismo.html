How to Get There

Sardinia has international airports in Cagliari, Alghero and Olbia. The drive from Olbia to La Cerra takes approximately one hour. A car is recommended because public transport in the area is limited.

With your own vehicle, Sardinia can also be reached by ferry. Useful arrival ports include Olbia, Porto Torres and Cagliari.

Rest Days and Other Activities

La Cerra

Follow the marked trails, take a mountain-bike trip or simply enjoy a quiet day at the agriturismo.

Monte Pulchiana

The prominent granite monolith offers wide views. The challenging summit trail requires experience and careful route finding.

Capo Testa

Explore remarkable coastal granite, walk through the Valle di Luna or discover the peninsula by canoe.

Costa Paradiso

Roughly 40 minutes away, the coast combines swimming and snorkelling with walking and granite exploration.

Lago del Liscia

Easy walks, birdwatching and ancient olive trees offer a quieter alternative about 30 minutes away.
