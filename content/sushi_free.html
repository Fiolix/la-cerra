Sushi-Free

The purpose of this small sector is to offer some relaxing bouldering and chilling on the green meadow - there is even a tiny creek. Approximately 15 minutes walking distance from the campsite you will be bouldering in the middle of a cow pasture. So - like everywhere else on La Cerra - please be considerate and respect both nature and the cows. If they come a little bit closer they are just curious about what is happening and don't want to harm anyone at all. The boulders are all concentrated within a very small area and quite exposed to the sun. Drop zones are perfect and heights are low to moderate. Grades are mostly about Fb 3 to 6b, which makes this sector suitable for children as well. Valentin's Day is located separate from the rest and offers some harder routes. We highly recommend Amor (Fb 6c) and Höppy End (Fb 7a).
