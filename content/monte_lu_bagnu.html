Bouldering › La Cerra › Monte Lu Bagnu
The bouldering area around Monte Lu Bagnu was first discovered and cleaned by Eadan Cunningham in 2018. Its six boulder groups can be reached within 35 to 60 minutes from the campsite or the day parking area. Access involves scrambling, overgrown macchia and some low-grade bouldering, so please approach this adventurous sector with care. Until individual topo images are available, every route is listed with a direct link to the corresponding point in Eadan's development videos.
