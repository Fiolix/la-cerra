Bouldering › La Cerra › Monte Lu Bagnu
The bouldering area around Monte Lu Bagnu was first discovered and cleaned by Eadan Cunningham in 2018. Eaden personally put a lot of effort in the whole area and first ascents are only listed if it was not him. It consists of eight different sectors that can be reached within 35 to 60 minutes from the campsite or the day parking area. The sectors Jumble and Bloc Meadow are fully described in this book since they are located directly next to the hiking trail and access is easy. Boulders are around 3 to 5 meters in height and drop zones are not always ideal but still quite good. The grading ranges mostly from Fb 5 to 7b but there are also a couple of easier routes for a nice warm-up. To get to the more distant sectors it takes some scrambling and strong motivation since some paths are heavily overgrown by macchia. Some piles of stones allow better orientation but please note that this is definitely not an easy walk and requires some low-grade bouldering even to get to the real problems - these are then graded from Fb 6b to 7c. So please be careful if you are going for this adventure! The problems of these sectors are listed in the printed guide but no pictures are provided. Instead, the guide links to video footage that tells the story of Eaden and how he developed this bouldering area.
