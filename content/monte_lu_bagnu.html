Bouldering › La Cerra › Monte Lu Bagnu
The bouldering area around Monte Lu Bagnu was first discovered and cleaned by Eadan Cunningham in 2018. It consists of eight different sectors that can be reached within 35 to 60 minutes from the campsite or the day parking area. Jumble and Bloc Meadow lie directly beside the hiking trail and have their own illustrated sector pages. Reaching the six more distant areas involves scrambling, overgrown macchia and some low-grade bouldering. Their problems are therefore presented below as direct links to Eadan's development videos.
