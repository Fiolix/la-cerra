La Cerra sector

Bloc Meadow

Bloc Meadow is one of the two easily accessible sectors around Monte Lu Bagnu. Located close to the hiking trail, it contains a varied collection of granite problems from easy, family-friendly climbing to technical and powerful lines around Fb 7a+. Most boulders are approximately 3 to 5 metres high.
