Bouldering › La Cerra › Bermuda Triangle
Bermuda Triangle is a small sector that has its charm because you can enjoy the view over La Cerra and the surrounding area while bouldering. Some of the boulders are marked with blue arrows and get a lot of sun due to the exposed location. This can be an advantage or disadvantage depending on the season. Because of the short approach, they are quite well suited for the morning or evening hours on hot sunny days. Drop zones are generally good even though some of the boulders are of considerable height. Although this sector is comprised of only a few boulders, the bouldering is quite diverse and grades range from Fb 5 to 6b. You will first pass Boulder 1, at which there is definitely potential for additional routes (check out the backside). Boulder 5 is located along the way to the upper sectors.
