Bouldering › La Cerra › Bermuda Triangle
Bermuda Triangle is a small sector with an open view across La Cerra and the surrounding countryside. Several boulders are marked with blue arrows and receive plenty of sun because of their exposed position. This can be welcome in cooler weather, while hot sunny days are better suited to a morning or evening visit. The approach is short, the landings are generally good and the climbing is varied, although some lines are relatively high. Boulder 1 is passed first and still offers potential for additional lines; Boulder 5 lies on the way towards the upper sectors.
